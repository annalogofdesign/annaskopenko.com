A little about me

I do my best work when I believe in the product and can help shape both its direction and its details.

I’m Anna, a product designer with a decade+ of experience building consumer products, from early-stage startups to companies serving millions.

At Fay, I grew from founding designer to leading a team of five while staying hands-on. Helping people access nutrition care and improve their health made the work especially meaningful to me.

I’m analytical, love a strong creative concept, and tend to sign up for the beta. I enjoy trying new tools and working with people who care enough to challenge each other and make things better.

On a career break

Somewhere in the Andes

I’m backpacking around South America, a trip I’ve been dreaming of for a while, and it’s finally happening. It’s a time to recharge, see some of the most beautiful places in the world, soak up new inspiration and work on personal side projects in between.

So far I’ve spent a few weeks in Peru: Lima, Huacachina, Arequipa, Colca Canyon, Cusco and the towns around the Sacred Valley, and the Inca Trail to Machu Picchu. Right now (end of October) I’m staying near Calca, which is where I finally shipped this site.

Next up: Bolivia, then Argentina and Chile, for lots of hiking, road trips and the best views of Patagonia! Once I’ve checked off a good chunk of my bucket list, I’ll be back in March with stronger legs and, hopefully, better ideas.
